## D1 = H5. `Reached::dying` has 19 entries, `ObjectKind` has 21

Both reports have it (deepseek 1, hydrogen 5). Judged once.

`model/tests/property.rs:1632` declares `dying: [u32; 19]`;
`Reached::count` (`property.rs:1729-1733`) indexes it with
`kind as usize` for every kind in `Census::dying`. `ObjectKind` has 21
values (`abi/src/lib.rs:324-351`): `MemoryControl = 19`, `Role = 20`.

Reproduced three ways.

1. Directed probe, with `start()` of `property.rs`, 40 endpoints retyped
   from `MEMORY`, then `CapDelete(own(12))`, then `Reached::count`:

   ```
   cap_delete memory_control: Err(Again); dying [MemoryControl, Untyped]
   panicked at model/tests/property.rs:1733:31:
   index out of bounds: the len is 19 but the index is 19
   ```

   The memory_control is the parent of every block (contract, "`memory_control`
   and its calls": "A block's capability is a child of the `memory_control`
   capability through which it was taken"), so everything taken from
   `MEMORY` is below it; 40 objects are more than the 32 units of one step.
2. Directed probe, 33 copies of the role in slot 19 (rights without
   `exec`), then `CapDelete(own(19))`, then `Reached::count`: `Err(Again);
   dying [Role]`, then `index out of bounds: the len is 19 but the index is
   20` at the same line.
3. The real generator: 10 seeds of 2,000 in the sweep (236, 340, 536, 606,
   774, 891, 1432, 1484, 1632, 1907) panic on this index. With the array
   widened to `ObjectKind::ALL.len()` the same ten seeds run to the end
   with every invariant holding; in them the memory_control stays dying
   for 1 to 2,249 steps (`dying[19]`), the role never.

Hydrogen's explanation of why the branch does not crash ("`Generator`
never creates 32+ copies of those capabilities") is wrong for the
memory_control: it needs no copies, only 32 objects made from its blocks,
which every run has after a few hundred steps. What keeps the 24 seeds safe
is only the rarity of the call (`property.rs:826`: one call in 74 × 300,
then one slot of 26, then the caller must be in the start-up space).
Deepseek's account is right.

Verdict: **real**. The counter does less than it says and the suite
panics on a reachable state; nothing is wrong in the model. Severity:
**should be fixed** before the merge, not merge-stopping: the branch's
24 seeds do not reach it, but any change of the generator or of `SEEDS`
has a 0.5 % chance per seed of turning the coverage suite into a panic.

Correction: `Reached` field `dying: [u32; ObjectKind::ALL.len()]`
(`[u32; 21]` derives `Default`). Test that fails without it:
`probe_memory_control_dying_reaches_count` above, kept as a test named
`dying_memory_control_is_counted` that asserts
`reached.dying[ObjectKind::MemoryControl as usize] == 1` after the probe.
Run with the fix: the memory_control probe counts `dying[19] = 1` (and
untyped 1), the role probe `dying[20] = 1`, no panic.

## D2 = H7. `dedup` without a sort counts one step several times

Effect on the branch's numbers, by mutation (`kinds.sort_by_key(|k| *k as

| pages | as shipped | sorted |
|---|---|---|
| 4 KB | `[28, 9475, 39, 12401, 141, 13, 13, 921, 474, …]` | identical |
| 16 KB | identical to 4 KB | identical |
| 32 KB | cspace 17,567, frame 26,151 | cspace 17,494, frame 25,774 |

1.5 % (frame). The floors are three quarters of the smallest of the three

Verdict: **real** (the field counts more than "steps after which an object
of each kind was dying"); severity **low**: no floor is wrong today. Deepseek's

Correction: in `Reached::count`, sort before `dedup`

`one step counted: [1, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 1, 0, 0]`, the probe passes.

## H6. Floors below what the directed cases guarantee

Hydrogen 6 only.

adds `sent += 1` and `sent_refused += 1` per seed (`property.rs:1859-1885`,

`moves_that_the_depths_rule_on_are_accepted_and_refused` passes, and the

fails only on the control, `dying_copies`, whose floor of 24 does guard
the random calls). The measured counts are `sent` 28, `sent_refused` 24,

The test file says what the floors are for: "A change of the generator

Correction: count the directed cases apart (`reached.sent_directed`), or

`sent_refused - 24 = 0`, so the honest floor is 0 and the generator must
be changed to reach a refused send at random, or the comment must say that
only the directed case reaches it. Check of the correction: the mutation

## What both reviewers missed

The sweep showed a contract hole. In 9 seeds of 2,000 (206, 363, 634,
789, 847, 956, 1267, 1675, 2029) the start-up thread moves the
memory_control capability (slot 12) into a capability space made from a
block, and invariant 21, "removal that never ends", fails at once.
Directed test: `start()`, a cspace of 128 slots retyped from `MEMORY`
into slot 4000 and grown, then `CapMove { source: own(12), destination:
SlotAddr::through(4000, 5) }`:

```
cap_move of memory_control into the space: Ok(Done)
check: Err("21: removal that never ends")
panicked at model/src/caps.rs:796:13: the walk of a removal does not end
```

(the panic is the next `CapDelete(MEMORY)`). The cycle is: the
memory_control is the parent of the block (contract, "`memory_control` and
its calls": "A block's capability is a child of the `memory_control`
capability through which it was taken"), the block is the parent of the
space's root, and the space holds the memory_control. The model applies
the contract: D-103 ("A capability space holds only what is deeper than
itself") restricts only "the root capability of another capability space"
and "an `untyped` capability", and `Model::fits` (`model/src/caps.rs:406-410`)
lets every other kind through. The proof under the rule relies on "Once
the walk reaches an ordinary capability it stays on ordinary ones,
because everything derived from an ordinary capability is a copy", which
the memory_control breaks: it is ordinary, and `memory_take` derives
untyped capabilities from it. Verdict: **the contract's fault**, severity
**stops the merge**: principle 4, "Every call is bounded", fails for any
holder of the memory_control with a `cspace` capability, and the node can
never finish removing that block. Correction, in the contract: "An
`untyped` or `memory_control` capability (a root or a copy) can be placed
there only if its depth is not smaller", and the proof's sentence
corrected (a memory_control, like an untyped, is restricted; its next
step is a derivation, so the walk's bound is unchanged). In the model,
`ObjectKind::Untyped | ObjectKind::MemoryControl => depth >=
self.depth(own)` in `fits`. Run: with that change the directed test
gets `Err(Invalid)` and the check holds, `cargo test --workspace` passes,
and the nine seeds run to the end. The directed test, kept in
`calls.rs` as `memory_control_is_not_placed_in_a_space_made_from_its_block`,
fails without the change. Two more lessons from the same sweep: the 24
seeds are too few to reach a state the generator reaches in 0.45 % of
seeds, so a periodic wider sweep (outside `verify`) would have found both
