### Finding 1 (High): `poll` returning the notify word without clearing it survives the entire test suite
- **Where**: [`model/src/ipc.rs:221-235`](file:///Users/btw/Documents/repos/dodos-kernel/model/src/ipc.rs#L221-L235) (`Model::wait`), [`model/tests/calls.rs:2108-2135`](file:///Users/btw/Documents/repos/dodos-kernel/model/tests/calls.rs#L2108-L2135), [`model/tests/calls.rs:3233-3243`](file:///Users/btw/Documents/repos/dodos-kernel/model/tests/calls.rs#L3233-L3243).
- **What is wrong**: Under the contract ([`kernel-interface.md:277-280`](file:///Users/btw/Documents/repos/Docs/architecture/kernel-interface.md#L277-L280)), `poll` behaves "As `wait`, but returns `would_wait` if the word is zero", and `wait` "Returns the word and clears it". Every test in [`tests/calls.rs`](file:///Users/btw/Documents/repos/dodos-kernel/model/tests/calls.rs) that calls `Syscall::Poll` on a non-zero notification word (`signal_sets_bit_0_or_the_bits_of_the_badge` at [`calls.rs:2125-2134`](file:///Users/btw/Documents/repos/dodos-kernel/model/tests/calls.rs#L2125-L2134) and `recv_with_a_reply_object_that_is_not_idle_leaves_a_pending_notification` at [`calls.rs:3241`](file:///Users/btw/Documents/repos/dodos-kernel/model/tests/calls.rs#L3241)) polls only once or immediately overwrites the same bit via `Signal` before polling again, never verifying that `Poll` cleared `notify.word`. Furthermore, Invariant 13 ([`src/check.rs:359-360`](file:///Users/btw/Documents/repos/dodos-kernel/model/src/check.rs#L359-L360)) only checks `notify.waiters.is_empty() || notify.word == 0`, which remains satisfied after `Poll` because `waiters` is empty.
- **How we know**: **Confirmed by a run** (Mutant `M115`, observable through the public API). Replacing `std::mem::take(&mut live(&mut self.notifies, id).word)` with a non-clearing read on `IfZero::WouldWait` passes all 95 unit tests (`--lib`), all 244 call tests (`--test calls`), and all 24 seeds × 5,000 steps of the property test (`--test property`).
- **Suggested test**: In [`tests/calls.rs`](file:///Users/btw/Documents/repos/dodos-kernel/model/tests/calls.rs), add `poll_returns_the_word_and_clears_it` (or assert in `poll_when_the_word_is_zero_is_would_wait_and_does_not_wait` at [`calls.rs:2158`](file:///Users/btw/Documents/repos/dodos-kernel/model/tests/calls.rs#L2158)):
  ```rust
  k.ok(Syscall::Signal(s(NT)));
  assert_eq!(k.go(Syscall::Poll(s(NT))), Ok(Output::Word(1)));
  k.no(Syscall::Poll(s(NT)), Error::WouldWait);
  ```
